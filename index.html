Short Bio

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Zhejiang University (2020–2025), advised by Prof. Huajun Chen at the ZJUKG-Lab. My research focuses on multi-modal learning and large language models, with an emphasis on AI for Medicine. I study how structured knowledge and verifiable feedback can improve reasoning and reduce bias and hallucination.

  • Multi-modal Learning & MLLMs: representation learning, reasoning and evaluation; tokenization, fusion and alignment with noisy, missing or imbalanced modalities.
  • Large Language Models: post-training, reinforcement learning and alignment; knowledge-augmented generation and agents.
  • AI for Medicine (current focus): medical models and clinical or biomedical agents; verifiable reasoning, research automation, and recursive self-improvement (RSI).

For an overview of structured knowledge in multi-modal learning, see our survey Knowledge Graphs for Multi-modal Learning: Survey and Perspective (Information Fusion, 2025)  [arXiv][repo].

I welcome research collaborations and discussions. Please feel free to get in touch.
